# Checklist truoc khi dua len CH Play

- Chon app id cuoi cung truoc lan upload dau tien.
- Tao icon, feature graphic va screenshot theo yeu cau Play Console.
- Dien Data Safety. Ban hien tai khong co dang nhap va khong gui du lieu nguoi dung.
- Neu gan quang cao that, cap nhat Data Safety va Privacy Policy theo SDK quang cao dang dung.
- Dung AAB da ky de upload len CH Play.
